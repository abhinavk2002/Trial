What counts as one of my cases

A row is flagged when it matches a strong term, or a supporting term together with an operative word (repair, plasty, correction…). Matching is fuzzy, so OCR slips like “chordec” or “hypospodias” still get caught. One term per line.

Data & privacy

Recognition happens with Tesseract running inside your browser — no image or patient detail leaves the device. The first extraction downloads the OCR engine and English language data from a CDN (~15 MB); after that the browser caches it and the app works offline. Records live in this browser's localStorage under this site's address only.

Built for a single-user thesis workflow. Verify every extracted value against the original list before using it as study data.
