Occipital lobe

Visual cortex and the two streams

  • V1, striate cortex (17) lines the calcarine fissure. The cuneus (above) represents the inferior field and the lingual gyrus (below) the superior field. The macula has a large representation at the occipital pole [2].
  • V2/V3 (18, 19) are association cortex. V4 (lingual/fusiform) handles colour; V5/MT (occipito-temporo-parietal junction) handles motion [5].

Ventral stream: "what"

Occipitotemporal pathway for identifying objects, faces, words and colour. Lesions cause visual agnosia, prosopagnosia, alexia and achromatopsia.

Dorsal stream: "where / how"

Occipitoparietal pathway for location, motion and visually guided action. Lesions cause Bálint syndrome, akinetopsia and optic ataxia.

References: [2] Blumenfeld · [5] Kandel · [35] Ungerleider & Mishkin 1982

Ungerleider and Mishkin proposed the two-streams model in 1982 from work in monkeys. It is the simplest way to sort the higher visual syndromes.
