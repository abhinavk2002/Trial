Primitive reflexes are normal in infancy and reappear when frontal inhibition is lost. Several (palmomental, snout) are also found in neurologically normal older adults, so on their own they mean little (Adams & Victor, DeJong). A unilateral grasp reflex is the most useful sign for localization.
