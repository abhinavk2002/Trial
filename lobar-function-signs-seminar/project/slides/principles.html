Introduction

Organizing principles of cortical localization

Functional hierarchy

Cortex runs from primary sensory/motor areas to unimodal association, heteromodal association, and paralimbic/limbic cortex. Lesions higher in the hierarchy cause complex, often bilateral-network deficits [14].

Hemispheric dominance

Language is left-sided in about 96% of right-handers and about 70% of left-handers (15% bilateral, 15% right) on Wada testing [40, 41].

Negative vs positive signs

Destructive lesions cause loss of function. Irritative lesions cause seizures whose first symptom marks the same cortex. Disconnection of two areas produces syndromes of its own [1, 25].

Tempo of the lesion

An acute stroke causes far more deficit than a slow-growing glioma of the same size and site, because plasticity lets function reorganize [1, 44].
