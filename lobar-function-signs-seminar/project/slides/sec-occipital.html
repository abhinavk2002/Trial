Occipital lobe

Vision: primary visual cortex (V1), visual attention and memory, and the dorsal "where" and ventral "what" association streams.

[1] Adams & Victor · [5] Kandel · [35] Ungerleider & Mishkin 1982

Blood supply: calcarine cortex from the PCA (calcarine branch). The occipital pole is a watershed between the PCA and MCA, which explains macular sparing [1, 2].
