Temporal lobe · Visual pathway

Meyer's loop and temporal resection

22–37 mm

Distance of the anterior tip of Meyer's loop behind the temporal pole (mean ≈ 27 mm) in Ebeling & Reulen's dissections [32]

  • Inferior optic radiation fibres, which carry the upper visual field, loop forward over the temporal horn [2, 4].
  • A lesion here gives a contralateral superior homonymous quadrantanopia ("pie in the sky"), often incongruous [4].
  • Even a standard anterior temporal lobectomy can cut the loop. Warn the patient about field loss and its effect on driving fitness [8].

References: [2] Blumenfeld · [4] Brazis · [8] Youmans & Winn · [32] Ebeling & Reulen 1988

The anterior extent of Meyer's loop varies a lot between people, which is why DTI tractography of the optic radiation is used before temporal lobe surgery in many centres.
