Neurosurgical application (1)

Grading eloquence

Sawaya grade I

Non-eloquent

Frontal or temporal pole, right parieto-occipital cortex, cerebellar hemisphere

Sawaya grade II

Near-eloquent

Near motor or sensory cortex, calcarine fissure or speech areas; corpus callosum; near the dentate nucleus or brainstem

Sawaya grade III

Eloquent

Motor, sensory, visual and speech cortex; internal capsule, basal ganglia, thalamus and hypothalamus; brainstem; dentate nucleus

In Sawaya's series of 400 craniotomies, neurological morbidity rose with grade [45]. The Spetzler–Martin AVM grade scores eloquence (sensorimotor, language and visual cortex, hypothalamus, thalamus, internal capsule, brainstem, cerebellar peduncles, deep cerebellar nuclei) alongside size and venous drainage [46].

References: [8] Youmans & Winn · [45] Sawaya et al. 1998 · [46] Spetzler & Martin 1986

Both grading systems simply formalize lobar localization: they ask whether the lesion sits in or next to cortex whose loss produces the signs covered in this seminar.
