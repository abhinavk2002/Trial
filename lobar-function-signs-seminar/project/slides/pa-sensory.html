Parietal lobe · Either hemisphere

Cortical sensory loss

Pain, temperature, vibration and light touch are relatively spared. Discriminative sensation fails in the contralateral limbs. These tests are valid only when primary sensation is intact [3].

Irritative sign: a sensory Jacksonian seizure, in which tingling marches along the sensory homunculus [1].
