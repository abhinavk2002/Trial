Cortical and deep territories of the cerebral arteries

ArteryCortical territoryDeep territory
Anterior cerebral (ACA)Medial frontal and parietal surface; a strip of superior convexity; medial orbital surfaceCorpus callosum except the splenium; caudate head and anterior limb of the internal capsule (Heubner)
Middle cerebral (MCA)Most of the lateral convexity of the frontal, parietal and temporal lobes; insula; temporal polePutamen, lateral globus pallidus, internal capsule, corona radiata (lenticulostriates)
Posterior cerebral (PCA)Occipital lobe; inferior and medial temporal lobe including the hippocampusThalamus, midbrain, splenium
Anterior choroidal (AChA)Uncus and parts of the medial temporal lobeLower posterior limb of the internal capsule, optic tract, part of the lateral geniculate body

The territories meet in watershed (border) zones. ACA–MCA: superior frontal convexity. MCA–PCA: temporo-parieto-occipital junction [1, 2].

References: [1] Adams & Victor · [2] Blumenfeld · [57] Rhoton 2002

Correction to a common slide error: the MCA does not supply the body of the corpus callosum. The ACA (pericallosal artery) supplies the callosum except the splenium, which comes from the PCA.
