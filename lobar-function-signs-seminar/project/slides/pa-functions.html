Parietal lobe · Parts and functions

Lateral and medial parts; lateralized functions

Parts

Lateral: primary sensory cortex; superior parietal lobule; inferior parietal lobule, made up of the supramarginal gyrus (phonological processing) and the angular gyrus (semantic word processing).

Medial: posterior paracentral lobule; precuneus (visuospatial imagery and orientation); posterior cingulate (emotion, motivation, memory networks).

Dominant

Calculation, language, skilled movement (praxis), reading and writing

Non-dominant

Scanning of space, construction, dressing, topographical orientation

Either side (lesion)

Cortical sensory loss, mild contralateral hemiparesis, homonymous hemianopia or inferior quadrantanopia ("pie on the floor")
