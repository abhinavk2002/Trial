Frontal lobe · Bedside testing

Frontal Assessment Battery (FAB)

1 · Similarities

Conceptualization: "How are a banana and an orange alike?"

2 · Lexical fluency

Mental flexibility: words beginning with "S" in 60 seconds

3 · Motor series

Programming: Luria fist–edge–palm sequence

4 · Conflicting instructions

Sensitivity to interference: "Tap twice when I tap once"

5 · Go / no-go

Inhibitory control: "Tap once when I tap once; don't tap when I tap twice"

6 · Prehension behaviour

Environmental autonomy: does the patient grasp the examiner's hands unbidden?

Each subtest scores 0–3, for a total of 18. Formal neuropsychology adds Trail Making B, Wisconsin Card Sorting and the Stroop test for dorsolateral function [6, 21].

References: [6] Mesulam · [21] Dubois et al. 2000 (FAB) · [1] Adams & Victor

Dubois and colleagues validated the FAB in 2000. It takes about 10 minutes and suits ward rounds and pre-operative baselines for frontal tumours. Record the raw score of each subtest, not only the total.
