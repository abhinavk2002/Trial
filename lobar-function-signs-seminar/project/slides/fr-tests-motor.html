Demonstrate fist–edge–palm three times, then ask the patient to do it alone for five cycles. In the reciprocal coordination test both hands rest on the table, one palm up and one palm down (or one fist, one open), and the patient switches them simultaneously. Strub and Black describe these tests in detail.
