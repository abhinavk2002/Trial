Take-home points

Five things to remember

  1. Localize from the pattern, not a single sign. Combine lobar signs with dominance and vascular territory [1, 2].
  2. Deficits (negative signs) and seizures (positive signs) point to the same cortex [1].
  3. Frontal and non-dominant syndromes stay silent unless you test for them: behaviour, executive function, attention, neglect and cortical sensation [6, 47].
  4. Primitive reflexes are non-specific in older adults; a unilateral grasp reflex is the localizing one [1, 3].
  5. Anatomy is a guide, not a guarantee. Individual variation and plasticity make functional mapping essential [42, 44].
