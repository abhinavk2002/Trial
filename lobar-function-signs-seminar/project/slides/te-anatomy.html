Lateral temporal lobe: superior, middle and inferior temporal gyri. Medial temporal lobe: hippocampus, parahippocampal gyrus, entorhinal cortex, amygdala, uncus and fusiform gyrus. The uncus and periamygdaloid cortex receive olfactory input, which explains olfactory "uncinate" auras.
