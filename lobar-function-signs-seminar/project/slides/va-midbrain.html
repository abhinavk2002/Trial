Vessels: paramedian and peduncular branches of P1/P2 and the basilar tip. Neurosurgically, Parinaud syndrome is the classic sign of a pineal-region mass or hydrocephalus [1, 4].

References: [1] Adams & Victor · [2] Blumenfeld · [4] Brazis

The general rule of brainstem localization: an ipsilateral cranial nerve palsy plus contralateral long-tract signs ("crossed syndrome") places the lesion at the level of that cranial nerve nucleus or fascicle.
