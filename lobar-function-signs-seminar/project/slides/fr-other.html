Frontal lobe · Other presentations

Other frontal presentations and the frontal pole

Motor-behavioural signs

  • Motor impersistence: cannot hold an act, such as keeping the eyes closed or tongue out
  • Motor perseveration: cannot stop or switch a movement
  • Echopraxia / imitation behaviour: copies the examiner's gestures [18]
  • Bladder and bowel incontinence without concern (medial frontal) [19]
  • Frontal dementia syndrome: bifrontal tumour, frontotemporal degeneration, hydrocephalus [1]

Seizures

Adversive (versive) seizures, focal motor seizures, epilepsia partialis continua and focal status epilepticus. A frontal tumour may present with any of these [1, 34].

Frontal pole (area 10)

Heteromodal cortex for metacognition ("thinking about one's own thinking"), empathy and social cognition. Lesions blunt insight and empathy [6].

References: [1] Adams & Victor · [6] Mesulam · [18] Lhermitte 1983 · [19] Andrew & Nathan 1964 · [34] Wyllie et al. 1986

Test motor impersistence by asking the patient to keep the eyes closed and the tongue protruded for 20 seconds. Test perseveration with alternating hand sequences or by asking the patient to draw alternating m-n loops (Luria).
