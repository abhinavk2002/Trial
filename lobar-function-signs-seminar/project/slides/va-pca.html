PCA: branches and syndromes

Segments: P1 (basilar tip to PCoA) · P2 (PCoA around the midbrain) · P3 (quadrigeminal) · P4 (cortical) [57].

Bilateral PCA / top of basilar: cortical blindness, Anton syndrome, Bálint (simultanagnosia), palinopsia, prosopagnosia, "gun-barrel" vision from bilateral macular sparing [1].

References: [1] Adams & Victor · [2] Blumenfeld · [56] Schmahmann 2003 · [57] Rhoton 2002

A PCA infarct can mimic an MCA stroke when the thalamic and peduncular branches are involved, causing hemiparesis and sensory loss as well as hemianopia. Look for the field defect.
