Parietal lobe · Hemineglect

Types of neglect and how to test them

Types

  • Personal: motor neglect (under-use of a limb that is not weak), premotor or intentional neglect (directional hypokinesia), and asomatognosia
  • Extrapersonal (visual): ignores objects and people on the neglected side
  • Representational: leaves out one side of a mental image. Bisiach's patients described only one side of the Piazza del Duomo, whichever way they imagined facing [53]

Bedside tests

  • Line bisection: the mark shifts toward the side of the lesion
  • Albert's cancellation test: lines on the neglected side are left uncrossed [54]
  • Clock or flower drawing: numbers crowd onto one side or half is missing
  • Double simultaneous stimulation: visual or tactile extinction

References: [7] Heilman & Valenstein · [23] Mesulam 1981 · [47] Strub & Black · [53] Bisiach & Luzzatti 1978 · [54] Albert 1973

Albert's test (1973) is a sheet of short lines scattered across a page; the patient is asked to cross every one. Neglect of the left side after right parietal damage is the classic pattern.
