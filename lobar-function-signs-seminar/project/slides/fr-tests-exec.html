Frontal lobe · Assessment (2)

Testing prefrontal function

Dorsolateral: executive function and attention

  • History: planning, keeping appointments, arriving on time, insight into the illness
  • Digit span forward and backward; months backward; serial 7s
  • Letter fluency (F, A, S) and category fluency (animals) in 60 s [47]
  • Trail Making A (attention) and B (set-shifting) [50]

Orbitofrontal: inhibition and conduct

  • Go / no-go test [21]
  • Stroop colour–word interference: name the ink colour, not the word [51]
  • Observe dress, social behaviour, empathy; interview the family

Abstraction

Proverb interpretation and similarities (look for concrete answers). Wisconsin Card Sorting Test: sorting by colour, form or number; frontal patients perseverate after the rule changes [52]. Block design and sorting tasks [47].

References: [21] Dubois 2000 · [47] Strub & Black · [50] Reitan 1958 · [51] Stroop 1935 · [52] Milner 1963

Interpret fluency scores against age- and education-matched norms rather than a fixed cut-off. Milner (1963) showed that patients with dorsolateral frontal excisions made more perseverative errors on the Wisconsin Card Sorting Test than patients with posterior excisions.
