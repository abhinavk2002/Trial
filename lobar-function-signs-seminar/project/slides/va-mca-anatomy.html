Vascular · Middle cerebral artery (1)

MCA segments and divisions

SegmentCourse and branches
M1 (sphenoidal)From the ICA bifurcation along the sphenoid ridge. Gives the lateral lenticulostriate arteries to the basal ganglia and internal capsule.
M2 (insular)Over the insula, after the bifurcation into superior and inferior trunks
M3 (opercular)Along the opercula to the Sylvian fissure surface
M4 (cortical)Cortical branches on the convexity

Superior division (frontoparietal)

Lateral orbitofrontal, prefrontal, precentral (pre-Rolandic), central (Rolandic), anterior parietal

Inferior division (temporoparietal)

Temporopolar, anterior / middle / posterior temporal, angular, posterior parietal

References: [2] Blumenfeld · [57] Rhoton 2002 · [58] Gibo et al. 1981

Gibo and Rhoton's M1 to M4 nomenclature is the one used in neurosurgery. In the source seminar M1 was described as the segment before the Sylvian fissure and M2 as the segment after it; the insular (M2) and opercular (M3) definitions above are more precise. Which division the parietal branches come from varies between patients.
