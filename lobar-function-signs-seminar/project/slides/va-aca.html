Vascular · Anterior cerebral artery

ACA: segments, branches and syndromes

Segments: A1 precommunicating (ICA to ACoA) · A2 infracallosal (ACoA to the rostrum/genu) · A3 precallosal (around the genu) · A4–A5 supracallosal [57].

Neurosurgical point: the recurrent artery of Heubner runs alongside A1 and is at risk during ACoA aneurysm clipping; its loss explains post-operative face–arm weakness and abulia. Some textbooks label the angiographic A1 as horizontal and A2 as vertical; the microsurgical segments above follow Rhoton.
