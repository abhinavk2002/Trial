Parietal lobe · Assessment

Bedside tests of parietal function

FunctionGraded tasks
PraxisPantomime to command, then imitation, then use of the real object; test each hand
Right–left orientation(1) on self, (2) crossed commands on self, (3) on the examiner, (4) crossed commands on the examiner
Finger recognition(1) non-verbal finger matching, (2) point to the examiner's named finger, (3) name fingers on self and examiner
CalculationVerbal simple, verbal complex, then written complex sums. Note the type: aphasic or alexic, spatial, or anarithmetria
ConstructionCopy a vertical diamond, a 2-D cross, a 3-D cube, a pipe, and a triangle within a triangle; draw a clock face
Geographical orientationLocate home and major cities on a blank map; describe a familiar route
NeglectClock drawing, line bisection, Albert's cancellation test

References: [3] DeJong · [7] Heilman & Valenstein · [47] Strub & Black · [54] Albert 1973

The graded right–left, finger-recognition, calculation and drawing tasks are those of Strub and Black's Mental Status Examination in Neurology. Errors in the dominant hemisphere point to the Gerstmann components; errors in drawing and maps point to the non-dominant hemisphere.
